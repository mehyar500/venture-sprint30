Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026 · MehyarSoft LLC

What we collect

  • Email address — provided at checkout, used to deliver your daily missions and receipts.
  • Purchase records — product, amount, and timestamps, tied to a private access token.
  • Progress data — which missions you check off on your dashboard.

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

What we use it for

  • Delivering the 30-day mission sequence you paid for (transactional emails only).
  • Running your dashboard and support.

We do not sell your data. We do not run marketing campaigns on your address — mission emails are product functionality, not a mailing list.

Unsubscribe

Every mission email carries a one-click unsubscribe. If you unsubscribe, you keep your dashboard access (token-gated) but stop receiving the daily emails.

Data retention & deletion

We keep purchase and progress records while your access is active. To delete your data, email team@mehyar.us with your purchase email and we will remove it.
