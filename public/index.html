FAQ

Straight answers.

What if I miss a day?
Nothing breaks. Your dashboard always shows your current day and every mission stays unlocked behind you. Miss a day, do two the next — the streak is a motivator, not a punishment.
Is this passive income?
No — and run from anyone who promises that. This is 20 focused minutes a day of real work: talking to buyers, writing offers, delivering work, raising prices. The "passive" part is that the system you build keeps working after the 30 days. The work itself is yours.
What if it doesn't work for me?
If you do the missions and reply when you're stuck, you'll get a straight answer — including "this lane isn't working, here's how to tell." If Sprint30 isn't for you, reply to any email within 14 days and we'll refund the $37. One email, no forms.
Do you actually read the replies?
Yes. Every mission ends with a reply prompt because your answers are the product working. Replies go to a human inbox and get human answers — usually same day.
I'm not technical. Will I keep up?
The missions use free tools you already know — docs, email, DMs, spreadsheets. If you can send an email, you can do every mission.
Why $37 and not free?
Free challenges get skimmed and abandoned. $37 is enough that you'll actually do day 4 — where the paid missions begin and the 30-day system kicks in.
